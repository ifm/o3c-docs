Electrical Integration Guide

System Overview

The O3C is a standalone integrated camera with on-board processing. All electrical connections — power, Ethernet, and I/O — are made directly to the camera unit via two pigtail connectors.

Connectors

The O3C has two pigtail connectors:

  • Power / I/O: M12 A-coded, 5-pin male connector on a 0.2 m pigtail

  • Ethernet: M12 X-coded, 8-pin female connector on a 0.2 m pigtail, 1 Gigabit

Power requirements

The O3C requires a 24 V DC power supply.

Ethernet

Connect the M12 X-coded pigtail to your host system via a 1 Gigabit Ethernet switch or direct link. The O3C provides a single Ethernet interface.

Note

CAN interface is not available on the O3C.

I/O

The O3C provides:

  • 1 × Hardware Trigger Input (Pin 2)

  • 2 × Switching Outputs (Pin 4, Pin 5) — driven by ODS application outputs

ESD and Cable Routing

Route data cables away from high voltage and high current sources such as motor drivers, inverters, and solenoids to minimize exposure to ESD.

Observe the minimum bending radius of the camera pigtail cables:

  • Fixed installation: 4 x cable diameter (4 x D)

  • Moving installation: 8 x cable diameter (8 x D)

For the 6.3 mm Ethernet cable, the minimum bending radius for fixed installation is 25.2 mm. Allow 50.4 mm of space for a complete 180-degree bend. These manufacturer limits differ from typical ifm guidance of 5 x D for fixed installation and 10 x D for moving installation.

Ground Loop Prevention

To prevent electrical interference:

  • Use insulating bushings (ifm E3R200) when mounting camera brackets directly to the vehicle chassis.

  • Avoid creating ground loops through the mounting structure.

  • See cable management guidelines for more detail.
