Manual calibration of ports for vehicle algorithms

The ifmVisionAssistant can be used to configure the camera pose for vehicle algorithms. Manual calibration defines the camera’s position and orientation relative to the user-defined vehicle or robot coordinate system.

Important

Perform this procedure only after the camera has been mounted in its final position. A change to the camera bracket, vehicle coordinate system, or camera orientation requires the calibration to be checked again.

Procedure

  1. Open Port settings and select Manual calibration of ports for vehicle algorithms.

  2. Select the camera port to calibrate.

  3. Select Rotate like a vehicle front camera when the camera is mounted in the standard forward-facing vehicle orientation. Otherwise, select the orientation that matches the physical mounting direction when viewed from the front of the camera.

  4. Enter the camera translation values. These are the distances from the origin of the user-defined vehicle or robot coordinate system to the camera reference point. Use the coordinate-system convention defined for the vehicle and enter values in the units shown by the interface.

  5. Review the rotation and translation values, then apply the configuration.

Validate the calibration

After applying the values:

  • verify that the camera orientation matches its physical mounting;

  • verify the translation values against the mechanical drawing or CAD model;

  • inspect the resulting vehicle-algorithm output; and

  • repeat the calibration if the camera or its bracket is moved.

For ODS, calibration errors can reduce detection performance. For a measured calibration using a checkerboard, use the Static Camera Calibration procedure instead.

Note

The ifmVisionAssistant is primarily a configuration and verification tool. Confirm that the calibrated values are stored in the device configuration before relying on them after a restart. See Changing parameters for configuration behavior.
