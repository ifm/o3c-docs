## Unbox and Connect the O3C

:::{image} resources/O3C.png
   :alt: O3C Camera
   :width: 30%
   :align: right
:::

The O3C is a standalone camera with integrated 3D, 2D, and IMU sensors; no separate processing unit or camera pairing is required.

## Before You Start

Prepare the following:

| Item              | Requirement                                                                                                        |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Power supply      | 24 V DC supply                                                                                                     |
| Power / I/O cable | M12 A-coded, 5-pin female cable; for example, [EVC070](https://www.ifm.com/de/en/product/EVC070)                   |
| Ethernet cable    | M12 X-coded, 8-pin male to RJ45, rated for 1 Gigabit/s; for example, [E12631](https://www.ifm.com/de/en/product/E12631) |

## Connect the Camera

1. Place the camera on a stable surface with the front unobstructed. Do not bend or strain the 0.2 m pigtail cables.
2. Connect the M12 A-coded power / I/O pigtail to the 24 V DC supply.
3. Connect the M12 X-coded Ethernet pigtail to the host computer or a 1 Gigabit Ethernet switch.
4. Apply power and wait for the camera to finish booting. When startup is complete, the PWR LED should be on. Refer to the [LED schema](../LED/led.md#led-schema).
5. Confirm that the Ethernet connection is established, then continue with the [software stack setup](index_sw_stack.md).

## Before Permanent Installation

For a bench setup, the steps above are sufficient. Before installing the camera on a vehicle or machine, use the following guides:

- [Mechanical Integration Guide](../IntegrationGuides/Mechanical/mechanical_guide.md): mounting position, clearance area, bracket design, and strain relief
- [Electrical Integration Guide](../IntegrationGuides/Electrical/electrical_guide.md): connector pinout, cable routing, bend radius, grounding, and LED behavior
- [Software & Controls Guide](../IntegrationGuides/SoftwareControls/software_controls_guide.md): network and software integration
- [Maintenance Guide](../IntegrationGuides/Maintenance/maintenance_guide.md): operation and service considerations
