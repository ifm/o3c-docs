# Maintenance guide

## Operating conditions

The O3C is rated IP67 and can be used in dusty or mixed indoor/outdoor environments with regular maintenance. The camera is designed for indoor use. Outdoor operation may be suitable depending on the application, but it must be benchmarked by the customer.

Dust and particles on the camera can gradually reduce measurement performance. Please validate against the deployment temperatures.

## Cleaning

Clean the front window of the camera housing regularly. Dirt, dust, or fingerprints can scatter the emitted light and reduce measurement accuracy, which may affect systems such as ODS.

- Use a clean, lint-free cloth, preferably microfiber, to avoid scratching the front window.
- Use compressed air or a blower brush to remove loose debris gently.
- Do not use water or harsh chemicals, which can damage the front window.
- Clean the camera at least weekly in non-dusty environments. In dusty environments, clean it daily or more frequently as required by the application.

## Field replacement

If an O3C unit shows visible damage or a software diagnostic indicates a hardware issue, the unit must be replaced.

:::{important}
Consult your vehicle or work-cell integrator before replacing a unit to ensure that calibration data is preserved or re-established correctly after replacement.
:::

The O3C is a standalone integrated device. Unlike the O3R, there is no separate VPU — all application configuration and calibration data is stored on the device itself. When an O3C unit is replaced:

1. The new unit requires reconfiguration and recalibration.
2. Extrinsic calibration must be re-run using the [Static Camera Calibration](../../CalibrationRoutines/SCC/README.md) procedure.
3. ODS configuration (zones, presets, ego-motion source) must be reapplied.

:::{note}
Always verify extrinsic calibration manually after hardware replacement.
:::
