# External Ego Data

## Overview

On O3C, the ODS application relies solely on host-provided ego-motion data —
the vehicle’s own velocity — for obstacle tracking relative to a moving
vehicle.

For the ODS integration, send the following information:

- `vx`: longitudinal velocity in m/s
- `vy`: lateral velocity in m/s
- `timestamp_ns`: timestamp in nanoseconds

The ego-motion data allows ODS to compensate for the vehicle's own movement between frames. Accumulating evidence over time in this way significantly improves detection performance: obstacles are detected earlier, tracked more stably, and spurious detections are suppressed.

Without valid ego-motion data, this propagation is not possible and the detection quality degrades accordingly. Supplying accurate ego-motion data that meets the requirements below is therefore essential for a well-performing ODS integration.

## Requirements for the ego-motion input

| Requirement         | Value                                                               |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Update rate         | At least 10 Hz, at most 20 Hz                                       |
| Velocity range      | `vx` and `vy` must be finite numbers within `[-10.0 m/s, 10.0 m/s]` |
| Maximum latency     | 500 ms (data must not be delayed by more than 500 ms)               |
| Maximum clock drift | 0.5 %                                                               |
| Clock jumps         | Permitted, but not more than once per minute                        |

:::{important}
    **Timestamp synchronization is not required.** The timestamps do not have to be
    synchronized with the camera, and NTP (Network Time Protocol) synchronization is
    not needed. However, each timestamp must be generated as close as possible to the
    actual measurement it belongs to. A timestamp taken long after the velocity was
    measured (for example, at the end of a processing chain) degrades the quality of
    the synchronization performed inside ODS.
:::

## Timing and synchronization requirements

The external ego-motion signal must be provided with low latency and a
measurement timestamp that is as close as possible to the actual motion event.
NTP synchronization with the camera is not required, but the signal must still
be time-accurate enough for ODS to align it with its internal motion estimate.

Until synchronization is established, the external ego-motion data is not used
for tracking. During startup or while the vehicle is stationary, the diagnostic
`ODS_VELOCITY_UNAVAILABLE` may remain active until the vehicle starts moving and
synchronization is established. If the input is delayed, irregular, or stamped
late, ODS performance is limited.

:::{warning}
    Complete and verify the extrinsic calibration of the camera head and the IMU
    before commissioning ODS. External ego-motion data does not replace this
    calibration. If the IMU is not calibrated correctly relative to the camera and
    the vehicle, ODS cannot reliably reconcile its own velocity estimate with the
    velocity provided by the host. ODS performance may be degraded, and repeated
    velocity-related diagnostics may be reported.
:::

## Troubleshooting

If `ODS_VELOCITY_UNAVAILABLE` or other velocity-related/ego-motion related
diagnostics occur:

1. Check whether the vehicle has moved since the last ODS startup. Synchronization
   only completes once the vehicle is in motion.
2. Verify that the input meets the [requirements](#requirements-for-the-ego-motion-input),
   in particular the update rate, the latency, and the timestamp quality.
3. Verify the extrinsic calibration of the camera head and the IMU, and repeat the
   initial standstill checks. Incorrect IMU calibration can cause the ODS velocity
   estimate to diverge from the velocity supplied by the host, even when the
   external ego-motion data is available.

## Data Format

| Field          | Type      | Unit  | Description                         |
| -------------- | --------- | ----- | ----------------------------------- |
| `vx`           | `float32` | `m/s` | Longitudinal ego velocity           |
| `vy`           | `float32` | `m/s` | Lateral ego velocity                |
| `timestamp_ns` | `uint64`  | `ns`  | Monotonic timestamp from the sender |

Binary payload order for `ODS_MOTION_DATA`: `<ffQ` (little-endian `float32`, `float32`, `uint64`).
