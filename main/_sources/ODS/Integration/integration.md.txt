# Integration

Integrating the O3C into an AGV or AMR spans three areas: **electrical**, **hardware/mechanical**, and **software**. Each area has distinct requirements and dependencies — addressing them in sequence reduces rework and avoids performance issues that are difficult to diagnose after the vehicle is assembled.

## Integration areas

| Area                     | Key topics                                                   | Details                              |
| :----------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------- |
| [Electrical](electrical) | Power supply, connector pinout, Ethernet wiring              | [Electrical integration](electrical) |
| [Hardware](hardware)     | Camera mounting, clearance areas, heat dissipation           | [Hardware integration](hardware)     |
| [Software](software)     | ifm3d API, ego-motion interface, diagnostics, occupancy grid | [Software integration](software)     |

## Recommended sequence

1. **Electrical** — Power and connect the O3C. Verify connectivity in the ifm Vision Assistant before any mechanical finalisation.
2. **Hardware** — Mount the camera at the correct height and tilt. Ensure clearance areas are respected to avoid stray light artifacts that degrade detection quality.
3. **Software** — Integrate the ifm3d API, implement the ego-motion feed, and wire up diagnostics handling in the vehicle control loop.

:::{important}
Diagnostics integration is not optional. The vehicle control system must monitor the ODS health status and stop the vehicle if the application group status is `critical`, `major`, or `not available`. See [ODS diagnostics](../../SoftwareInterfaces/ifmDiagnostic/diagnostic.md).
:::
